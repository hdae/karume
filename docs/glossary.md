# Glossary

> Term → meaning summary → pointer to the canonical source. The Japanese used in conversation is in
> [GLOSSARY.ja.md](GLOSSARY.ja.md).
> **The canonical meaning is the source each row points to**; this table is only an index (a
> definition is not kept in two places).
> When a summary and its source disagree, the source wins and this table is fixed.
> When a new term enters the docs, add one row here.
> The full list and status of the quantization methods themselves are indexed by
> [quantization](quantization.md).

| conv3d w8a8 / `conv8`                | The integer dot-product path of conv3d: int8-resident weights × activations quantised to int8 **once per input pixel over its channels**, one exact i32 sum per tap folded into f32 by one `fma` per tap (`convCompute: "a8"`; preset token `conv8`). | w4a8                                 | The integer dot product path of resident i4 weights × per-token i8 activations. | Integer GEMV (`integerGemv`)         | The opt-in decode GEMV of QAT linears whose activation arrives as packed int8 SRQ codes: the products of the weight and activation codes are summed **exactly** per group (lane sums in f32 within 2^24, then an i32 tree) and the scales are applied once, in a fixed epilogue order. | Byte-plane GEMV (`linearGemvPlanes`)  | A `reorder` strategy of linear, run only through the private Session key `[REORDER_STRATEGIES]` (`linearGemvPlanes: true`) where linear is at the level `reorder`: the decode GEMV of i2 linears with f32 activations: `lanes` threads share an output channel and a lane group holds `rows` channels, and the weight words are split into byte planes that `unpack4x8unorm` and an exact magic-number conversion turn into the values −2..1. Eligible only at measured shapes (the QAT E2B lm_head, 262144 × 1536) | ADR [0141](decisions/0141-byte-plane-gemv.md), `packages/runtime/src/kernels/linear-gemv-planes.ts` |

| fake-quant                           | Rounding weights to the quantization grid and keeping them as f32. | Quality measurement (`quality.json`) | The record left in the series: the difference (quantization error) between the f32 output before rounding and the output of the rounded weights, measured with torch on CPU at export. | Extended atomic layer    | Non-core ops in the IR vocabulary that **cannot** be written as an exact equivalent from other ops of the vocabulary (old layer 1′).
