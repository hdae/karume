# Speed-up recon: the answer check on the RTX 5070 Ti

Question (from the 3080 Ti record, §11): run the runbook `outputs/diag/perf-recon-2026-10-24/runbook/` on the 5070 Ti in
one sitting, and settle which of the bit-identical candidates measured on the 3080 Ti transfer to Blackwell / D3D12, the
F-2 route, and the post-F-1 baseline.

## 1. Summary

- **The sitting is clean.** No slow mode, the memory clock at P0 in every loaded sample, 0 uncaptured errors, 0 slow-mode
  re-runs in 152 guarded cases. The card's limit is its ~300 W power cap, not heat (§3).
- **Every rewrite measured is bit-identical on D3D12.** The exceptions are by design: the fused2 contraction probe is
  bit-equal on D3D12 but not on Vulkan (so FMA contraction differs between the backends), and C6 / V-8 changes numbers
  (§4).
- **F-1 holds on D3D12.** Both scenario hashes are unchanged. The 33f DiT forward went 2,604.9 → 2,383.3 ms, the request
  327.3 → 304.6 s (§5).
- **Attention: K-84's form transfers stronger than on Ampere** (×1.68 self, ×2.20 cross, ×1.69–1.72 window, two runs):
  −422 to −432 ms per 33f forward. No register cliff within 48 added registers in either run. Today s16 is ahead on
  Blackwell; with K-84 the online route is ahead at 33f (by 55–59 ms) and at 121f (by 2.24 s, one run). F-2 reduces to
  landing K-84 on Blackwell (§6).
- **Linear: K-85's L1c skeleton is refuted on Blackwell** (×0.41). Production already runs at 92–97% of the dp4a-acc
  rate (cross-part estimate); only a K64 geometry re-sweep (×1.07, −72 ms per forward) is left (§7).
- **Small ops: the measurable compliant set is −135.4 ms per 33f forward**, the upper end of the estimate. K-87 is about
  0 for the norms; F-8's strided-copy pathology does not exist on the 48 MB L2 (§8).
- **VAE: the bit-identical stack is about −14.1 s per 33f stage** (part of it estimated); SK-4 is refuted; C6 / V-8 is
  stronger (×4.7 on the next chunk's conv2d) (§9).
- **Host: H-38 −1.52 s, L-26 −0.50 s, L-28 −0.85 s, L-29 about −2.3 s per request.** D3D12 cold pipeline compile adds
  about +0.58 s per request (§10).
- **LLM decode: LD-1 + LD-3 take a gemma4 E2B token from 11.73 to 6.87 ms** (×1.71); K-90 is the large part. K-88's f32
  GEMM rewrite mostly does not transfer (×1.03–1.07) (§11).
- **Additive estimate: −72.7 to −75.2 s per 33f request** (−23.9 to −24.7% of the 304.569-s post-F-1 request). It lands
  inside the earlier −18 to −30%, with a different mix (§12).

Exceptions, both by design:
- **fused2** (the contraction probe of the 3080 Ti record §6) reads 0 on D3D12, against 2,317 / 2 / 2,555 differing words
  on Vulkan (S04 `checks[2,3,10,11]`). FMA contraction therefore differs between the backends. The compliant rewrites
  are bit-identical on both backends measured, so "bit identity rests on the driver contracting `acc + v*v`" is a Vulkan
  statement. [Speculation] DXC (`/Gis`) contracts neither form; the file cannot tell that from "both alike".
- **C6 / V-8** changes numbers (int8 conv2d): relRms 3.9e-3 on synthetic inputs (S05 `parts[0].result.c6`).

Across machines the outputs differ (D3D12 / Blackwell vs Vulkan / Ampere: the step-01 hashes, the VAE chunk hashes, the
gemma4 `readDigest`), while the generated gemma4 text is equal. That is what per-environment sha rows expect (ADR 0106).

  samples. Step 01's burst check is vacuous for quick-check (2 steps).
- The step files do not record the backend; D3D12 is inferred from Chrome on Windows. Step 03a's reference brackets are
  older-round values.
