# 0053: conv1d as implicit GEMM (groups == 1, bit-identical)

- Status: accepted (2026-08-13 — main-session decision under the maintainer's approval of
  performance phase 1, perf-ledger K-4a); decision 5 re-graded to MAY on 2026-10-18.
- Date: 2026-08-13
- Scope: the `conv1d` kernel (`packages/runtime/src/kernels/conv1d.ts`, fragments shared with
  `gemm.ts`, the route choice in the executor). IR, contract and exporter unchanged (no re-export).
- Related: [0024](0024-conv2d-implicit-gemm.md) (the 2D original) /
  [0022](0022-gemm-register-blocking.md) (GEMM skeleton).

## Context

The dacvae codec's direct conv1d took 1,758.96 ms (decoder + encoder), 30% of Irodori's GPU time, at
577 GFLOP/s — the limits ADR 0024 diagnosed for conv2d. A spike routing conv1d through the conv2d
igemm as H = 1 kept the WAV sha256 and ran 13.4–16.2× faster. Numbers:
[research/2026-08-13-op-timing-restats.md](../research/2026-08-13-op-timing-restats.md) §3, §5, §8.

## Decision

1. **The 1D form of ADR 0024**: `C[Cout, N] = W[Cout, K] × Xcol[K, N]` with `M = Cout`, `N = Lout`,
   `K = Cin·K`. The A tile (the weight flattened is `[M, K]` row-major), bias-first, store and inner
   loop are **the same fragments** as in 2D (`prologueAConv` / `fillAConv` / `convAccInit`; existing
   ops' generated bytes did not change — the snapshots detect it). Only the B tile is new: the 1D
   `xcol` gather and the split of flat k into `(ic, k)` (`conv1dKDecode` in `gemm.ts`).
2. **Bit-identical (MUST)**: ascending flat K equals the direct kernel's ascending `(ic, k)` loop;
   bias is the initial `acc`; out-of-range x is 0 (ADR 0024 decision 3). The only difference is signed
   zero, reachable only when `bias[oc]` is a literal `−0.0` and every later tap product is `−0.0`
   (under round-to-nearest `x + (−x) = +0.0`). The 143,356 conv1d bias elements of the time held no
   `−0.0`; a negative parity case (bias `−0.0`: direct `0x80000000`, igemm `0x00000000`) pins the
   mechanism for future models.
3. **Batch is the dispatch z axis** (ADR 0024 decision 2). The uniform carries six 1D words
   (`channels_in` / `length_in` / `kernel` / `stride` / `padding` / `dilation`). `length_out` is not
   carried: it equals `dims.n`, and one fact is not split over two words (dead fields are prohibited — ADR
   0022 decision 5 / ADR 0024 decision 2).
4. **v4 is chosen by `kFlat % 4 == 0 && Lout % 4 == 0 && stride == 1`** (`conv1dUsesVec4`, one
   place); the 2D distinction of `Wout % 4` and `N % 4` disappears. v4 for stride > 1 is a future
   candidate (encoder downsampling at stride 2 / 8 / 10 / 12).
5. **igemm covers groups == 1 — the implemented subset.** groups > 1 (the 12 depthwise convs of the
   SBV2 front end) stays on the direct kernel (`conv1d:v2:…:direct`), the **permanent A/B
   bit-comparison oracle** (`packages/runtime/tests/gpu_conv1d_parity_test.ts`: 13 shapes with an
   exact Uint32 match, a rerun with the 32-row m tile forced, the signed-zero case, a check of the
   route keys run). Extending igemm to groups > 1 MAY be done if the direct kernel stays as the
   oracle. (Re-graded 2026-10-18 to MAY: groups == 1 matched the demand of the time; ruling: accepted
   as proposed.) The igemm key, first `conv1d:v3:…`, is now
   `conv1d:v4:f32:igemm{tileM}x{tileN}{v4}:wg{x}x{y}{:w…}{g…}`, derived from the geometry
   (`packages/runtime/src/kernels/conv1d.ts:6`, `:140`; `g` is the i4 group of ADR 0069 decision 5).
6. **The i8 scale is per row = output channel** (ADR 0024 decision 6). Detectors: the i8 parity
   shapes with two or more m tiles (Cout = 96 / 66).
7. **The m-tile predicate is the shared `conv2dIgemmMTile`** (a pure function of M = Cout; a 1D copy
   would put one boundary in two places). Tile waste for Cout < 32 (SBV2 decoder 16, waveform head 1)
   is known and not addressed. The dispatch limit fails loudly (Lout ≤ 65,535 × 128 ≈ 8.39 M samples
   ≈ 175 s at 48 kHz — `docs/limitations.md`).

## Acceptance (2026-08-13)

RTX 3080 Ti / Vulkan, full suite green; reordering flat k to `(k, ic)` turns two parity checks red.
conv1d 1,758.96 → 110.1 ms (16.0×), Irodori GPU time −27.4%, WAV sha256 unchanged (restats §8,
commit `7a725d7`).
